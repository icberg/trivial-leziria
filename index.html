One screen, one Game Master, the whole class racing from Santarém to the European Parliament.

Choose how many teams are playing. Teams take the names of the real European Parliament political groups that Portuguese MEPs sit in, each with one “Ask the group” lifeline.

Give each team a name if you like (optional) — the party colours and the ⓘ button stay as a guide to who's who.

How to play (30-second version)

Teams roll the die and move along the road. Colored squares ask a question of that category — the Game Master reads it aloud, the team answers out loud. Correct: stay. Wrong: return to where the turn started. Gray squares draw an event card. Purple squares force a trilogue: two teams negotiate a deal and answer together. Questions get harder in each country row. Reaching Brussels needs an exact roll — then one final plenary question, category chosen by the other teams, decides the game.

Each team may use “Ask the group” once per game to turn a question into multiple choice.
